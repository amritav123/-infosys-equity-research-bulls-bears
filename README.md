## Executive Thesis: Priced for Zero Growth With a Safety Net
At ₹1,108.80, the market prices Infosys for a perpetual **-0.11% annual decline in USD free cash flow**, overlooking contracted growth drivers and strong cash returns:
* **Downside Floor:** Even assuming zero perpetual USD FCF growth (with 3.0% annual INR depreciation), a reverse-DCF yields **₹1,156/share** (+4.3% above CMP).
* **Contracted Growth:** A signed **$2.0B+ 7-year Financial Services mega-deal** ($285.7M/year run-rate) begins ramping in late Q4 FY27, adding **+1.09 percentage points** to FY28E USD revenue growth.
* **AI Cannibalisation Stress-Tested:** Modeling a 0.50x cannibalisation ratio (vs. 0.49x observed in Q2 FY27) yields a base DCF value of **₹1,254/share**. Even under a severe 1.00x cannibalisation stress test, intrinsic value holds at **₹1,204/share** (+8.6% above CMP).
* **Shareholder Payouts:** With ₹34,674 Cr in net cash (post-interim dividend) and an **85% FCF payout policy**, FY28E capital returns are projected at **₹31,069 Cr** (₹76.50/share).
