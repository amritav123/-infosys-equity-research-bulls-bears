## 1. Project Overview

This project presents an independent equity research analysis and financial valuation of Infosys Limited, one of India's leading IT services companies.

The objective is to assess whether Infosys is undervalued by the market amid concerns surrounding artificial intelligence (AI), revenue cannibalisation, pricing pressure and increasing competition.

The research combines fundamental analysis, financial modelling, industry analysis, peer benchmarking and multiple valuation methodologies to develop an investment recommendation.

## 2. Investment Thesis

**Investment Recommendation: BUY (BULL)**

The central investment thesis is that the market is pricing Infosys for almost zero perpetual US dollar free cash flow growth, despite its existing client commitments, AI-related opportunities and strong cash generation.

Key investment highlights:

- **Valuation:** Infosys trades at 15.5x trailing earnings compared with a peer median of 19.7x.
- **Contracted Growth:** A Financial Services client commitment exceeding $2 billion over seven years provides visibility into future revenue.
- **AI Transition:** Infosys Topaz generated $540.2 million in Q2 FY27, representing 10.5% of revenue.
- **Cash Generation:** Strong free cash flow supports an 85% shareholder payout policy.
- **Downside Protection:** Sensitivity analysis tests the impact of AI cannibalisation and lower deal conversion.

Companies considered for competitive benchmarking:

- Tata Consultancy Services (TCS)
- HCL Technologies
- Wipro
- Tech Mahindra

The analysis considers revenue growth, operating margins, deal wins, employee attrition and valuation multiples.

## 6. Risk Analysis

The investment thesis is stress-tested against three major risks:

**1. AI Cannibalisation**

AI-led services could replace existing traditional IT revenue. The model tests different cannibalisation ratios to evaluate their impact on intrinsic value.

**2. Deal Conversion Risk**

Large contract wins may not translate into expected revenue. The analysis incorporates conservative assumptions regarding the conversion of new deal value into revenue.

**3. Margin Pressure**

Wage inflation, pricing concessions and AI-related efficiency sharing could affect operating profitability. The financial model incorporates these costs into its EBIT margin projections.

## 7. ESG Analysis

The research also considers environmental, social and governance factors, including:

- Employee attrition and workforce stability.
- Gender diversity and workforce representation.
- Renewable energy usage and emissions.
- Board independence and corporate governance.

ESG factors are considered as part of the broader business risk assessment and are not assigned a separate valuation premium in the DCF model.
